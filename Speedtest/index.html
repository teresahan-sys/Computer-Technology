Cakes

Cakes are sweet baked foods made from flour, sugar, and eggs. They evolved from ancient flat breads into rich, festive desserts enjoyed worldwide.

Key Types of Cakes

    Shortened cakes: Butter or oil makes them moist and dense (like pound cakes).
    Unshortened cakes: Whipped eggs make them light and airy (like sponge cakes).
    Layer cakes: Stacked tiers with fillings and icing, popular for parties.
